The Pipeline

Six Python modules, orchestrated by GitHub Actions, running on the 1st of every month at 9:30 AM IST.

1
auth.py

Authenticate

Posts credentials to Kite's login endpoint, answers the TOTP challenge, and captures the enctoken cookie used for every later call.

2
portfolio.py

Fetch Holdings

Pulls equity holdings, mutual fund (Coin) holdings, and open positions from the Kite API.

3
prices.py

Enrich Data

Each holding is enriched with live market data from Yahoo Finance — current price, P/E, sector, 52-week range.

4
research.py

Gather Research

Pulls FII/DII flow data, downloads brokerage PDFs from ICICI Direct and HDFC Securities, and collects macro indicators as grounding context.

5
analyst.py

Generate Report

Portfolio data plus live research context goes to Gemini, which returns an 11-section research-grade analysis in markdown.

6
emailer.py

Send Email

The final report is wrapped in a responsive HTML template and delivered to your inbox via Gmail SMTP.

What You Get

A comprehensive, AI-generated investment report covering every angle of your portfolio.

01

Portfolio Snapshot

Total value, invested capital and P&L, split across equity and mutual funds.

02

Equity Holdings

Every stock with qty, avg cost, CMP, value, P&L, and a HOLD / ADD / TRIM / WATCH verdict.

03

Mutual Fund Holdings

Coin holdings with invested versus current value and returns per fund.

04

Winners & Losers

Top three performers and bottom three, each with a one-line explanation.

05

Sector Allocation

Sector-by-sector exposure as a share of the portfolio, mutual funds included.

06

FII/DII Flows

Foreign and domestic institutional flows in ₹ crores, and what they mean for your book.

07

Market Context

India macro (RBI, inflation, policy) and global drivers (Fed, DXY, Brent, geopolitics).

08

Research Highlights

Key takeaways from the brokerage reports pulled in this month, cited by source.

09

Key Holdings Review

Deep dive on every position above 5% of the portfolio, with a valuation call.

10

Action Items

Three to five specific moves for the month, each with its rationale.

11

Watchlist

Two or three stocks and funds worth tracking, with target entry prices.

Get Started in
5 minutes.

Clone, configure your secrets, and let GitHub Actions handle the rest.

1

Clone & Configure

git clone https://github.com/akamaurya/portfolio-monitor.git
cd portfolio-monitor
cp .env.example .env
# Add your Kite, Gemini, and Gmail credentials
2

Add GitHub Secrets

Go to Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions and add KITE_USER_ID, KITE_PASSWORD, KITE_TOTP_SECRET, GEMINI_API_KEY1, GMAIL_ADDRESS, GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD and RECIPIENT_EMAIL.

3

Sit Back

The pipeline runs automatically on the 1st of every month. You can also trigger it manually from the Actions tab.
